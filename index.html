About

I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Psychological Sciences at Rice University, working with Dr. Tianjun Sun. I received my PhD in Social and Personality Psychology from Washington University in St. Louis in 2026, advised by Dr. Joshua J. Jackson.

I study personality dynamics: how behavior varies across situations and over time, and how people differ in these patterns. I develop Bayesian, psychometric, and machine learning methods for intensive longitudinal and language data.

Research

More about my research

  • Person and situation dynamics

    Bayesian models of how behavior varies across situations and over time, and of how people differ in these patterns.

  • Psychological measurement

    Adaptive tests, models of how items work across situations, and smartphone tests of cognition in daily life.

  • AI and LLMs

    Gaussian process and language models for individualized assessment, life course prediction, and auditing bias in AI interviews.
